Our Story

A Promise, Kept Quietly

What began as easy conversation, late and unhurried, grew into a certainty neither of them announced but both of them knew. Hizaam remembers the exact street corner; Saheeka remembers what was said, not the corner — and between the two memories, the whole story fits.

Two families, once separate tables at gatherings, now find themselves planning one wedding — and are delighted about it. What follows is a week of small ceremonies, old customs, and the two of them, finally, side by side.
